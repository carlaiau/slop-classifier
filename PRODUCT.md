## Platform
web

## Users and purpose
General readers inspecting pasted English articles, essays, and informational prose. Research asks whether JEV offers useful sentence detection at live-reading speed, and whether readers understand its limits.

## Confirmed constraints
The approved research plan governs the implementation. Detection, latency, and reader understanding are separate outcomes. Word shading is conditional. No document verdict, typing-time scoring, public deployment, or fabricated research results. Paid inference is opt-in with durable budget accounting. The participant pilot requires passing technical gates.

## Brand and interaction commitments
Inherit ../read-with-jev's warm paper, serif reading column, quiet controls, sentence highlights, and whole-text navigation. The reader is an instrument for inspection. Preserve original text and distinguish unscored, pending, scored, insufficient, and failed states. Keyboard access and mobile configuration/map drawers are required.

## Evidence
The supplied PDF is a proposal, not validation. OpAI-Bench is a revision-provenance dataset. Synthetic fixtures and demo scores are software checks only. No live detector evaluation or participant study has been completed here.

## Implementation choice
A local Node.js 24 ESM server and browser-native modules keep the prototype and research harness small. The official JEV JavaScript SDK stays server-side. No sibling project files are modified.
